SAMPLE OUTPUT · NOT A CUSTOMER REPORT

Evidence before investment.

This illustrative brief shows how AutomationScan is intended to turn business inputs into a decision-ready discovery plan. All names, figures and findings below are fictional.

01

Decision question

Should the business optimise the current application landscape, modernise a core platform, or complement it with specialist capabilities?

02

Evidence register

User-stated process observations are separated from uploaded records, sourced vendor claims, calculations and assumptions.

03

Key gap

Application renewal dates and actual licence utilisation are incomplete. This is an evidence gap—not proof of overspend.

04

Scenario set

Stay / optimise, modernise / extend, complement and replace are compared against the customer's stated requirements.

05

Validation gates

Confirm integration effort, security controls, localisation, process fit, commercial terms and migration complexity through evidence.

06

90-day action plan

Collect application and spend records, validate the highest-impact process, run supplier demonstrations and document decision gates.

IMPORTANT

This is a sample, not proof of product accuracy.

A real brief should be generated from the organisation's own evidence and clearly distinguish facts, calculations, assumptions and external claims. Vendor information must be checked against current documentation and contractual terms.
