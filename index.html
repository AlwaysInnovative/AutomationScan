QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Before you start

A few important things to know about the assessment.

What is AutomationScan?

A free discovery tool for understanding repetitive business work and identifying automation opportunities worth investigating.

Is this an AI consultant?

No. The public scanner is a browser-based rules engine with lightweight text signal extraction. It does not claim to understand your business like a human consultant would.

Does it automate anything automatically?

No. It helps you see and prioritize opportunities. Your business decides what to implement, with which tools and at what cost.

Is the score a guarantee?

No. It is a directional signal based on your inputs, not a promise of savings or a technical automation assessment.
