AUTOMATIONSCAN TRANSFORMATION METHOD™

Business first. Evidence before technology.

AutomationScan is designed to structure transformation discovery—not to manufacture a vendor winner from incomplete information.

METHOD

From business context to decision readiness.

The method separates facts, calculations, hypotheses and validation requirements.

01

Discover

Capture industry, business model, operating model, scale, geography, goals and current applications.

02

Baseline

Map processes, pain, customisation, integrations, spend and measurable outcomes.

03

Map

Separate ERP capabilities from specialist applications, workflow platforms, data and integration layers.

04

Assess

Identify maturity, application health, regulatory considerations, automation opportunities and transformation scenarios.

05

Compare

Generate an investigation set from industry and capability signals. This is not a procurement ranking.

06

Validate

Turn uncertain claims into scripted demos, POCs, reference checks, architecture evidence and migration rehearsals.

07

Quantify

Use customer-supplied assumptions to model TCO, opportunity signals and business-case scenarios.

08

Decide

Compare Stay/Optimise, Modernise/Extend, Complement and Replace against the evidence.

09

Plan

Translate the selected direction into discovery gates, migration readiness, roadmap and governance.

EVIDENCE STANDARD

What AutomationScan will not pretend to know.

  • Unverified vendor capabilities are labelled for validation.
  • Missing catalogue evidence is not treated as proof that a capability does not exist.
  • Savings are not presented as guaranteed ROI.
  • Customer references are not invented.
  • Benchmark figures are not fabricated.
  • Regulatory content is a consideration checklist, not legal advice.
  • AI/agentic claims should be tested against demonstrated workflows, controls and measurable outcomes.
  • Vendor or partner relationships should not secretly change the customer-specific analysis.
