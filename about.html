ABOUT AUTOMATIONSCAN

Practical visibility for better business decisions.

AutomationScan is an independent discovery tool built by AlwaysInnovative to help teams document operational work, transformation requirements, evidence and decision questions before committing to technology or implementation.

What it does

The platform helps users move from business context and process evidence toward automation opportunities, market-fit questions, requirements, vendor investigation, economics, POC readiness and roadmap considerations.

What it does not do

It does not guarantee savings, recommend a supplier without validation, replace procurement or security review, or make investment decisions for a business. Built-in examples and vendor information are directional and should be validated against current supplier evidence.

Methodology

AutomationScan separates business evidence from technology choices. Inputs are retained through the assessment journey, decision calculations use configured rules, and recommendations are intended to be traceable to the information supplied.
