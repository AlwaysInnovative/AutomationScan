AUTOMATION GUIDE

Real estate automation: reduce repetitive coordination around leads and properties.

Real estate workflows often combine leads, calls, messages, documents, calendars and CRM records. Mapping the handoffs can reveal practical automation opportunities.

Lead follow-up

Identify standard enquiry acknowledgements, reminders and task creation. Keep nuanced buyer or seller conversations under human control.

CRM administration

Look for repeated entry of lead, property and activity information across systems. Define a single source of truth before automating updates.

Property documents

Map document collection, naming, routing and status notifications. Preserve approval and compliance checkpoints.

Scheduling

Recurring viewing coordination and reminders can often be structured around availability rules, while exceptions remain manual.

Reporting

Track recurring pipeline and activity reports. Automate repeatable preparation only after confirming the underlying data is reliable.
