AUTOMATION GUIDE

Logistics automation: make repeated coordination visible.

Logistics operations can involve many status updates, documents, spreadsheets and system handoffs. Start by mapping the repeated coordination work.

Shipment updates

Identify repeated status checks and notifications. Define the source of truth and what happens when tracking information is unavailable or inconsistent.

Documents

Map recurring document creation, collection, naming and routing. Keep compliance and approval checkpoints explicit.

Data entry

Look for repeated copying between transport, warehouse, finance and customer systems. Prioritize clean, structured handoffs.

Customer notifications

Standard milestone updates can be structured around defined events, while delays, disputes and exceptions may need human communication.

Operational reporting

Document recurring reports, their source data and validation. Automate preparation only after data ownership is clear.
