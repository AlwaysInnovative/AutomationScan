AUTOMATION GUIDE

Healthcare automation: focus on administration, not replacing care.

Clinics can use process automation to reduce repetitive administrative work while keeping clinical judgment and sensitive decisions with appropriate people.

Scheduling and reminders

Map appointment requests, confirmations, reminders, rescheduling and no-show follow-up. Identify where standard rules can reduce manual coordination.

Administrative intake

Look for repeated form handling, document routing and status updates. Avoid placing confidential health information into general-purpose assessment tools.

Patient communications

Separate routine, approved messages from conversations that require clinical context or individual judgment.

Reporting

Identify recurring operational reports such as appointment volumes, outstanding tasks and administrative queues. Document the source and review process.

Human review remains important

Automation should support defined administrative workflows while appropriate professionals retain responsibility for clinical decisions, exceptions and sensitive communications.
