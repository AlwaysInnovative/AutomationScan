AUTOMATION GUIDE

Accounting automation: where repetitive finance work can hide.

Accounting teams often work across email, spreadsheets, accounting systems, documents and approvals. The first step is mapping the repeated movement between them.

Invoice processing

Look for repeated extraction, validation, coding, approval routing and posting steps. Keep exception handling visible rather than hiding it inside an automation.

Payment follow-ups

Recurring reminders and status checks can be structured around clear rules, while unusual disputes or sensitive customer situations may need human review.

Reconciliations

Identify recurring matching and exception queues. Separate predictable matches from items requiring accounting judgment.

Reporting

If the same inputs produce the same management report each period, document the data sources, transformations and review steps before automating generation.

Controls matter

Finance automation should preserve approvals, audit trails, access controls and review responsibilities. Faster processing is not a substitute for financial control.
