AUTOMATION GUIDE

Professional services automation: give delivery teams more visibility.

Consultancies, agencies and other professional-services teams often lose time to project administration, reporting, email and document coordination.

Project administration

Map recurring status updates, task creation, reminders and document handling around each project.

Client communication

Identify standard updates and follow-ups that follow predictable rules. Keep advice, negotiation and nuanced client communication human-led.

Reporting

Recurring project and management reports are candidates for structured preparation when the source data is consistent.

CRM and pipeline admin

Look for repeated updates after meetings, proposals and status changes. Define ownership so automated updates do not create duplicate records.

Protect delivery quality

Automation should reduce administrative friction without removing the review points that protect client outcomes.
